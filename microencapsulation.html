What it means

Protecting an ingredient
inside a formulation.

Microencapsulation surrounds an ingredient with a protective coating to create small capsules. In product development, this can be explored to help manage stability, handling or release characteristics.

Beevenom Leonitus has described this area in connection with cosmetic products. The actual performance of a formulation depends on its composition, testing and intended use.
