Our story

Years of hands-on work.
One careful step at a time.

We started producing bee venom in 2014. As our work grew, we developed collection equipment designed around practical experience with bee colonies.

Our company reports a network of 43 cooperators and annual production of around 10 kg. We supply purified bee venom to partners in the European market.
