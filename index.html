Our story

Rooted in beekeeping.
Built around better collection.

We have produced bee venom since 2014. Alongside production, we developed our own collection technology to support efficient work with bee colonies.

Today, our network includes 43 cooperators and produces around 10 kg of bee venom per year. We supply purified bee venom to partners in the European market.
