Questions about
the collector.

Answers restored from the original Beevenom Leonitus site. Product details and collection guidance can change; contact us to confirm the current specification before ordering or use.

Collection equipment
What are the collector’s dimensions?

The original product description lists dimensions of 420 × 510 × 150 mm. Please confirm the current version and hive compatibility with us before purchase.

Which hive type does the dome fit?

The original site describes the dome as suitable for Langstroth hives. Check fit against your hive and the current collector instructions.

What temperature is recommended?

The original site states that collection should be performed above 15 °C. Conditions vary; follow current instructions and local beekeeping guidance.

When during the day should collection take place?

The original site recommends collecting in the morning before bees leave the hive or in the evening after they return.

How much venom can be collected?

The company’s published figures report approximately 2–3.7 g from 10 hives in about 25 minutes. These are reported results, not a guarantee; actual yield varies.

How often can I collect?

The original site suggests an interval of four days between collections. Confirm the current guidance and consider colony welfare and local beekeeping advice.

How should collected venom be stored?

The original site recommends a dark container and storage below 0 °C. Ask for current handling and documentation guidance for your specific material.

Does the set include glass plates and a battery?

The original description includes glass plates. It says a battery is not included and specifies a 12 V source; confirm the current set contents before ordering.

Do I need to adjust the electronic settings?

The original site advises against changing the factory settings. Follow the current manufacturer instructions and contact us if you need support.
