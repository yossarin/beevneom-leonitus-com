About bee venom

Collected with care.
Prepared for partners.

Bee venom, also known as apitoxin, is a complex natural secretion produced by honeybees. It contains several bioactive components and has been the subject of scientific study.

Our business focuses on producing purified bee venom and discussing product requirements with professional partners. We can share information about current lots and available documentation on request.

Research and context

What research explores

Traditional use

Bee venom has a history of use in traditional apitherapy. Historical use does not establish safety or effectiveness for a medical condition.

Scientific study

Researchers have studied bee-venom components in laboratory and clinical settings. Findings are specific to the study and do not mean a product is an approved treatment.

Our purified bee venom is a specialist raw material. Contact us to discuss handling information and documentation for your professional requirements.
